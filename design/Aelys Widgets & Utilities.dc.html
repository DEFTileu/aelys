M · Messages
Messages: a chat app with AI drafts
A separate app under Apps › Communicate, opened at /apps/messages. "Needs reply" is the work queue. In each chat, Aelys's draft sits above the input, and Send moves you straight to the next chat. You can also write yourself; messages Aelys sent are marked. Settings › Telegram keeps only the connection and the rules. Try it: Send, Edit, Skip, and your own message with Enter.

S · Settings

These are the sections that exist in the code: Appearance, Weather & location, AI & models, Telegram, Connection, Security. Each setting is a row with the label and one line of explanation on the left and the control on the right. Rows are grouped into cards. Changes save immediately; the only Save button is for text fields (URL, token). All panels are clickable.

1 · Widget sizes
Every size shows different content
Square cell 264 × 264, gap 16. Three sizes only: S 1×1 (264 × 264), M 2×1 (544 × 264), L 2×2 (544 × 544). The size is chosen by the user — never by record count. Bigger is not "more rows"; each size answers a different question.

2 · Customize
Edit in place. One gesture per action.
Drag the card body to move. Drag the corner to resize; it snaps to S / M / L with a live outline. Tap "−" to remove. No arrows, no size dropdown, no Auto. Keyboard: focus a card, arrows move, Shift + arrows resize, Delete removes.

Add widget sheet
Opened from "Add widget" in Customize and from the Widgets entry in the sidebar. Pick a widget, pick a size (default M), Add.
